Food & Agriculture of Gilgit

Gilgit's cuisine is hearty and relies on local produce. Agriculture shapes local diets and traditions.

Famous Foods

Chapshuro

Meat-stuffed flatbread baked over an open flame.

Goshtaba

Lamb meatballs cooked in yogurt gravy, served on festivals.

Apricot Jam

Sweet jam made from locally grown apricots.

Giyaling

Traditional local bread made from wheat or barley.

Customs & Festivals

Harvest celebrations and local fairs are common. Hospitality is a key cultural value.

Local Sayings
"A guest in the house is like a blessing from God — always offer tea and a warm smile."
